# 🍕 Zomato Profitability Analysis — FY22 to FY24

![Dashboard Preview](/output/dashboard_preview.png.png)

## 📌 Project Summary

A complete data-driven strategy analysis of Zomato's 
profitability journey from FY22 to FY24. This project 
analyses food delivery turnaround, Blinkit unit economics, 
and the cross-subsidy structure using real annual report data.

**Central Question:** Is Zomato's Blinkit bet a justified 
long-term growth play — or is it delaying profitability?

**Answer:** Blinkit is a justified bet. Food delivery is 
already funding it with a ₹528 Cr surplus in FY24.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Metric | FY22 | FY23 | FY24 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| Food Delivery EBITDA (₹ Cr) | -766 | -10 | +912 |
| Blinkit EBITDA (₹ Cr) | — | -1,016 | -384 |
| Consolidated Revenue (₹ Cr) | 4,192 | 7,079 | 12,114 |
| Cash Reserves (₹ Cr) | — | 7,782 | 12,241 |
| Food Delivery CM/order (₹) | 7 | 18 | 22 |
| Blinkit loss/order (₹) | — | -85 | -19 |

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Continue Blinkit expansion** — unit economics improving 
   on a predictable curve. Loss/order down 78% in one year.
2. **Protect food delivery margin floor** — must stay above 
   5% of GOV to sustain self-funding structure.
3. **Leverage Hyperpure** as Blinkit's supply chain backbone 
   to reduce replenishment costs.
4. **Launch Blinkit ad platform** — ₹3/order ad revenue = 
   ₹609 Cr additional annual revenue.
5. **Target 25 Mn monthly transacting users** by FY25 — 
   current growth of 25% in 2 years is too slow.
6. **Expand Going-Out** in top 8 metros only — near-zero 
   delivery cost, high margin potential.
